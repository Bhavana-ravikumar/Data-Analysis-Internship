# Data Analysis Internship Projects | Cognifyz Technologies

Restaurant data analysis completed during my Data Analysis internship at Cognifyz Technologies. I completed two of the three program levels (Level 1 and Level 3), each with a Python notebook and a Power BI report.

## Projects at a Glance

| Level | Focus | Python | Power BI |
|-------|-------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Cuisines, cities, price ranges, online delivery | `Level1_python.ipynb` | `Level1_PowerBI.pbix` |
| 3 | Rating text, votes, price range vs delivery and table booking | `Level3_python.ipynb` | `Level3_PowerBI.pbix` |

## Dataset
A restaurant dataset of about 9,500 restaurants across many cities, with cuisines, price range, ratings, votes, and online delivery and table booking flags. It was provided as part of the internship and is not included in this repository.

## Level 1
- **Top cuisines:** found the three most common cuisines and the share of restaurants serving each
- **City analysis:** found the city with the most restaurants and the average rating for each city
- **Price range distribution:** charted and calculated the share of restaurants in each price range
- **Online delivery:** calculated the share offering delivery and compared average ratings with and without it

## Level 3
- **Rating text analysis:** cleaned the rating text labels and counted the most common words
- **Votes analysis:** found the most and least voted restaurants and the correlation between votes and rating
- **Price range vs delivery and table booking:** compared delivery availability, table booking and average rating across price ranges

## Tools
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Power BI, Jupyter Notebook
